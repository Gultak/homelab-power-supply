## The project

- **Goal:** a 3D-printed 1U 10" rack case that distributes power to the homelab:
  - 54 V for two PoE switches
  - 12 V for four devices
  - 5 V for one device, plus an AUX 4-pin Molex
- **Owner:** based in Ireland, so prices and suppliers in EUR for Ireland. They order from
  DigiKey Ireland, pooling with friends to reach free delivery (€92.25 inc. VAT).
- **Repo:** [Gultak/homelab-power-supply](https://github.com/Gultak/homelab-power-supply). The
  board lives at the repo root:
  - KiCad 7 board, generated by `build.py`; `post.py` fills zones and runs DRC
  - gerbers
  - `README.md` and `BOM.md`
  - `digikey-bom.csv`
  - `docs/`: renders `top-r7.png` and `bottom-r7.png`, assembly PDF, DRC report

  The old v1 board (DC-DC brick) has been removed, and there is no "v2" naming any more.
- **Repo workflow:**
  - Make a branch per change and open a PR. Commit as `Gultak <claude@gultak.de>`.
  - Merged PRs: #1–#19.
  - When the board images change, bump the render file names (`-r7` → `-r8`), because
    GitHub caches README images by file name.
- **Toolchain:**
  - Board generation: KiCad 7 `pcbnew` Python. Run `python3 build.py && python3 post.py`.
  - Exports: `kicad-cli` makes the SVGs, gerbers, drill files and PDF; `rsvg-convert` and PIL
    turn the SVGs into PNG renders on a black background, with the bottom-side silkscreen drawn
    in white.

## The board, as it stands

- **Size and build:** 83.2 × 80 mm, 2 layers, 2 oz copper, 1.6 mm. Four M3 holes at
  (4, 4), (79.2, 4), (4, 75.5) and (79.2, 75.5), each with a 9 mm copper keep-out.
- **Inputs:** two 6-pin JST VH (B6P-VH), pins 1–3 positive and 4–6 GND.
  - J1, 54 V from the LOP-300-54, at the top end of the left edge.
  - J10, 12 V from the LOP-300-12, at the top end of the right edge.
- **Outputs:** 2-pin 5.08 mm screw terminals (YE0001XC321, Phoenix MKDS-1,5 footprint), with
  the wire entry at the board edge.
  - Bottom edge, left to right: 5V (J8), then 12V 1–4 (J4–J7).
  - Left edge, bottom end: 54V 1 (J2), 54V 2 (J3).
  - J9 is the 5 V AUX Molex 8981 on the top edge, far left. Its pinout is +5V, GND, GND, +5V,
    which is not the PC standard.
- **54 V:** a fused passthrough in the left column.
  - Fuses F2/F3: 5×20 mm, 2.5 A time-lag (Schurter 0031.8201 holders).
  - It has its own ground (GND54), isolated by a 1.5 mm gap. No tie jumper (JP2 was removed).
- **12 V:** fuses F4–F7 are ATM 5 A (Keystone 3568 holders). F8 (5 A) feeds the Pololu.
  C5/C6 are 470 µF 16 V polymer capacitors (16SEPG470M).
- **5 V:** Pololu D24V90F5, soldered flat between the inputs.
  - Mounting: 2× 4-pin 0.1" headers in the end rows of small holes, plus solid wire links
    through the four large holes, which carry the current. No standoffs.
  - Gap above the board: 2.5 mm minimum, about 5 mm preferred.
  - Orientation: VOUT end on the left, and the FB/PG/EN pin column towards the front (output)
    edge. GND is the large hole on the column side.
  - Check that last point against the labels on the real module before soldering. The pinout
    came from Pololu's drawing, and its first reading had VIN and VOUT swapped.
- **Status LEDs:** about 10 mA each. They are case-mounted Lumex SSI-LXMP059ID LEDs on
  2.54 mm Dupont headers D2–D4, with the series resistors on the board:
  - R1 5.1 kΩ 2512, 1 W (0.53 W)
  - R2 1 kΩ 1206
  - R3 300 Ω 1206
- **Pololu standoff holes:** removed. The 6 mm copper keep-outs around the former M2 holes are
  gone along with them.
- **DRC:** 0 electrical violations, 0 unconnected. The only remaining items are silkscreen
  overlaps and library notices.

## 54 V distribution to the PoE switches (decided, not yet built)

- **Connector:** Anderson Powerpole PP15 on both ends, replacing the 5.5/2.1 barrels on the 54 V
  side only. 12 V stays on 5.5/2.1 barrels, so a 54 V lead can never reach a 12 V device.
- **Parts:**
  - Housings 1327 (red) and 1327G6 (black), or 1327G16 (yellow) for + to mark 54 V.
  - Closed-barrel 15 A contacts for 16–20 AWG (1332).
  - Optional panel clamp 1462G1.
  - Standard housings, not finger-proof (the two types don't mate).
- **Panel mount in the printed case:**
  - A tight pocket, about 16 × 8 mm plus roughly 0.2 mm clearance, with the front of the housing
    sticking out by about the mating depth (around 10 mm; measure it).
  - One printed retainer plate behind each pair, with a slot for the wires, held by two M3
    screws into heat-set inserts in printed bosses.
  - Glue or a hook for pull-out retention.
- **Leads:** 20 cm, crimped yourself (cheap PP15/30/45 ratchet crimper) or soldered.
  Red = +54 V, black = GND.
